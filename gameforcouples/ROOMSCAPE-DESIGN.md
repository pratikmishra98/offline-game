# Roomscape multiplayer design

Friends join the same room over a local network. A match has three short rounds with the same movement and character controls. Each round gives players a different way to win.

## Round 1 · House Brawl

- Players start with about 30 seconds to search the house for items or powers. Slap, Kick, and Puff give everyone moves to use regardless of what they find.
- When the timer ends, players meet in the arena circle and compete to stay alive. Hits lower health. Health kits spawn during the round, so players can race for a refill or try to beat an opponent to it.
- A player is eliminated when their health runs out. The last player standing wins the round.

## Round 2 · Cushion Crown

- One golden cushion appears in the middle. Grab it and run away from the other players.
- Players can knock the cushion loose and steal it back. The player with the most time holding the cushion when the round timer ends wins.

## Round 3 · Moving Day

- A safe circle travels through the house. An arrow shows where it is headed.
- Players earn one point per second inside the circle. They can bump each other out. Anyone outside can run back in and keep scoring.
- The player with the most points when time runs out wins.

## Playtest suggestions

- Flash the next health kit location a couple of seconds before it spawns so players have time to react.
- Give the heavy kick a visible windup so opponents can dodge it.
- Slow the cushion holder slightly. Count total time over all their holds so stealing it back still matters.
- Show match standings between rounds. One proposed score awards three stars for first place, two for second, and one for third. Most stars after three rounds wins. A short ring-out tiebreak is an option.
- Tune each round's timer, item effects, starting health, and match scoring with friends once the basic loop is playable.
